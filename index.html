About ADAL

Welcome to the Agentic and Dependable AI Lab (ADAL), an undergraduate research lab in the Department of Computer Science at Elizabethtown College. Students in ADAL build agentic AI systems and study how to make AI dependable: secure, private and reliable, from edge devices to the cloud. Our current research focuses on four areas:

  • AI for cybersecurity: machine learning for malware detection and network traffic analysis
  • Edge AI and IoT systems: real-time AI on low-power and heterogeneous hardware, from smart-home malware detection to at-home exercise coaching for older adults
  • Large language models and AI agents: conversational AI agents and a self-hosted LLM for Elizabethtown College
  • Privacy-preserving machine learning: federated learning and differential privacy, such as predicting student success without sharing raw records

ADAL students have co-authored papers at IEEE and ACM venues, presented at the ACM SIGCSE Student Research Competition, and gone on to Ph.D. programs and industry careers. The lab’s research has been supported by Intel, Google, the National Science Foundation, and Elizabethtown College’s SCARP and Faculty Grant programs.

The lab, formerly the Advanced Data Analytics Lab, is led by Peilong Li, Ph.D., Associate Professor of Computer Science and Program Director of the Computer Science and Data Science Programs. Learn more on his personal website.

What to expect from ADAL

  • Work on real research problems in AI, security and computing systems alongside faculty
  • Earn credit through a Signature Learning Experience (SLE) such as a senior project or capstone
  • Join as a paid summer researcher through SCARP, or as a research assistant during the semester ( how)
  • Present and publish your work, and build experience for graduate school or a career in AI, data science and cybersecurity

Projects

Self-Hosted LLM for Elizabethtown College

Student research internships to prototype a self-hosted large language model system for the college (2026-2028).

JayQuery: Privacy-Preserving Student Success Prediction

Vertical federated learning to predict student success without sharing raw records across campus offices (SCARP 2026).

ARISE: AI-Powered At-Home Exercise for Older Adults

AI-powered, real-time interactive exercise support for elderly communities on low-power edge devices (SCARP 2025).

RydeeNLP: Personalized Japanese Learning with NLP

Proficiency-adaptive Japanese learning through NLP-powered translation and a browser extension.

JayRadar: A Zero-Code Vision System for FIRST Robotics

An open-source, coprocessor-agnostic vision pipeline for the FIRST Robotics Competition (SCARP 2023).

IoTSec: Scalable, Real-time Malware Detection for Smart Environments

Edge-network malware detection with deep learning for smart homes and communities (Google Cloud Research Award 2022-2023).

NetSec: Accelerated Malware Detection with Machine Learning

Machine-learning pipelines for detecting malware, supported by SCARP 2022 and an Intel Academic Grant.

DataScope: Predictive Diagnosis in IIoT-enabled Smart Manufacturing

Data science for predictive diagnosis in industrial IoT (IIoT)-enabled smart manufacturing, with industry mentors (SCARP 2021).

ANTA: Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning for Network Traffic Analysis

A performance study of machine learning and deep learning models for detecting encrypted malware traffic (SCARP 2020).

Etown Crawler

An AI-driven crawler rendered in 3D that self-teaches itself to collect rewards.

ACETA: Encrypted Network Traffic Analytics

Classify encrypted network traffic with accelerated machine learning models.
